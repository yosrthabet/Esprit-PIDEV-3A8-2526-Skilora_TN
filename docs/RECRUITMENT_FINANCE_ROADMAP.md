# Recruitment to Finance Roadmap

## Current State

- Job discovery/search is active.
- Employer job posting and offer management are active.
- Candidates can apply and employers can review applications.
- Interview scheduling and hire offers are the bridge into Finance.

## Target Flow

Employer posts job -> Candidate applies -> Employer reviews application -> Employer plans interview -> Employer sends hire offer -> Candidate accepts -> Finance contract is created -> Escrow/payment begins -> Delivery/review closes the contract.

## Phase 1: Application Pipeline

- Employer sees applications as a candidate pipeline.
- Employer can mark as viewed, move to interview, reject, or send offer.
- Candidate can track application status.

## Phase 2: Interview Scheduling

- Employer can schedule online, onsite, or phone interviews.
- MVP meeting links use generated Jitsi links or pasted external links.
- Later integrations can add Google Meet or Zoom API.

## Phase 3: Hire Offer

- Employer sends salary, currency, contract type, start date, and benefits.
- Candidate accepts or rejects.
- Accepted offers become the canonical source for finance contracts.

## Phase 4: Finance Bridge

- Accepted hire offer creates a finance contract.
- Employer funds escrow or creates payment intent.
- Freelancer delivery and employer approval release payment.

## Deferred Integrations

- Google Calendar / Meet API.
- Zoom server-to-server OAuth app.
- WhatsApp/SMS notifications.
- Community conversation thread per job.
- Formation recommendations based on rejected/missing skills.
